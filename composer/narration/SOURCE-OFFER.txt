Lensmith Composer narration image: licences and source offer
=============================================================

This image adds optional narration to Lensmith Composer. Most of it is under permissive licences:
kokoro-onnx and onnxruntime (MIT), soundfile (BSD-3-Clause) with libsndfile (LGPL-2.1). espeakng-loader 0.2.4
states no licence (its project has since added MIT). Every package in the narration runtime is listed with its
licence in THIRD-PARTY.md (https://github.com/lensmith-ai/composer/blob/main/THIRD-PARTY.md). The Kokoro-82M model
and voices (Apache-2.0) are NOT in this image; Composer downloads them into your data volume only when you turn
narration on.

espeakng-loader 0.2.4 bundles the eSpeak NG speech library, version 1.52.0, which is licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 or later. Its licence text is in this folder:
eSpeak-NG-1.52.0-COPYING.txt.

phonemizer-fork 3.3.2, which kokoro-onnx uses, is also licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or
later. It is pure Python: its complete source is the package installed in this image
(/venv/lib/python3.13/site-packages/phonemizer/), and its licence text is
/venv/lib/python3.13/site-packages/phonemizer_fork-3.3.2.dist-info/licenses/LICENSE. It is also available from
https://pypi.org/project/phonemizer-fork/3.3.2/#files.

Source offer. For as long as we offer this image, the complete corresponding source of eSpeak NG 1.52.0 and the
espeakng-loader 0.2.4 source distribution that built the bundled library are attached to the same GitHub
Release as the image (https://github.com/lensmith-ai/composer/releases), at no charge. They are also available
from their projects:
  - eSpeak NG 1.52.0: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/archive/refs/tags/1.52.0.tar.gz
    (SHA-256 bb4338102ff3b49a81423da8a1a158b420124b055b60fa76cfb4b18677130a23)
  - espeakng-loader 0.2.4: https://pypi.org/project/espeakng-loader/0.2.4/#files
If you cannot get them from either place, open an issue at https://github.com/lensmith-ai/composer/issues
and we will send them to you.
